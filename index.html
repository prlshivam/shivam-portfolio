01 — ABOUT

About Me

I am a PhD researcher working in Theoretical Condensed Matter Physics at the Physical Research Laboratory (PRL), Ahmedabad.

My research focuses on unconventional magnetic orders and the relationship between crystal symmetry, magnetic symmetry and electronic structure.

In particular, I am interested in altermagnetism and momentum-dependent spin splitting, with the broader goal of understanding unconventional routes to spin-polarized electronic states.

02 — RESEARCH

Research Interests

Understanding how symmetry can generate unconventional magnetic phenomena and momentum-dependent electronic structures.

01

Unconventional Magnetism

Exploring magnetic orders beyond conventional ferromagnetism and antiferromagnetism and their consequences for electronic structure.

Altermagnetism

Studying a class of unconventional magnetic materials exhibiting spin splitting without conventional ferromagnetic net magnetization.
